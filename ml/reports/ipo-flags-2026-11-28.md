# Prospectus red flags and IPO outcomes — 28 Nov 2026

305 mainboard IPOs listed Jul 2020 – Nov 2026 whose red herring prospectus is on SEBI's site were scanned with the 12 rules of the prospectus checker (`backend/stocksense/rhp.py`). 277 have six months of trading to judge.

Median listing-day gain +11.5%; median six-month excess return after listing -4.6%; 45% were weak (six months more than 10 points behind NIFTY 50).
